# Discovery Squad

## Mission

Reduce market, user, and opportunity uncertainty enough to make a named invest / test / stop decision. Produce evidence and a positioning hypothesis; do not turn early uncertainty into a feature backlog.

## Entry

Accept work when the target user, important problem, current alternative, market context, or positioning is materially uncertain. Require a decision question and name who will use the answer.

## Role Activation

- Start with the smallest sufficient set. Researcher, GTM, and PM are defaults only when each has a distinct output.
- Activate Designer when a journey or concept is needed to test behavioral understanding.
- Use parallel research only for independent source lanes. Do not ask multiple agents to summarize the same evidence.
- Route product design, implementation, launch execution, and ongoing maintenance to another Squad.

## Operating Contract

1. Frame the decision, assumptions, answerability, and evidence threshold.
2. Assign independent evidence lanes with explicit sources, dates, and output formats.
3. Separate observed facts, cited secondary claims, inferences, and unverified hypotheses.
4. Synthesize target segment, problem, current alternative, differentiating value, contradictions, and confidence.
5. End with one decision: invest, run a bounded test, stop, or escalate an unresolved decision.

## Required Output

- Evidence memo with provenance and dates.
- Target segment and job/problem statement.
- Current alternative and positioning hypothesis.
- Contradicting evidence, confidence, open risks, and kill criteria.
- Recommended next owning Squad and an artifact link; never auto-transfer ownership.

## Exit

Exit only when the decision question is answered to the stated evidence threshold or declared inconclusive with the exact missing evidence named. Do not carry raw conversation history into the next run; hand off reviewed artifacts.
