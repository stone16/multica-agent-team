# Growth Squad

## Mission

Launch the product and improve acquisition, activation, retention, and commercial learning through bounded experiments. Produce decisions from measured behavior, not activity volume or launch theater.

## Entry

Require a product or proposition, target segment, positioning hypothesis, named funnel problem or market test, live measurement source, and decision owner. Send unresolved target-user or positioning questions to Discovery.

## Role Activation

- GTM and PM are defaults only when the work includes both a market bet and a product or metric decision.
- Activate Designer for acquisition, onboarding, activation, or retention experience changes.
- Activate Researcher for a named evidence gap, not generic market scanning.
- Activate Engineer for instrumentation or a bounded product experiment after the metric contract is accepted.
- Activate Evaluator when instrumentation integrity or an outward claim requires independent verification.

## Operating Contract

1. Establish the dated baseline with numerator, denominator, segment, and source.
2. Define one hypothesis, one primary metric, guardrails, a decision threshold, and kill criteria before action.
3. Prove or kill one channel or funnel constraint before opening another.
4. Package external actions for human approval; agents do not publish, send, spend, or launch irreversibly without authorization.
5. Measure the result, identify confounders, and choose scale, revise, stop, or return to an earlier Squad.

## Required Output

- Positioning, launch, or experiment brief with audience and current alternative.
- Baseline, instrumentation definition, hypothesis, metric, guardrails, budget, duration, and kill criteria.
- Result with numerator, denominator, dates, segment, and confidence.
- User or market feedback with provenance.
- Explicit scale / revise / stop decision and recommended next owning Squad.

## Exit

Exit when the predeclared decision threshold or kill criteria is reached, or when the run is declared inconclusive with the missing evidence named. Never reuse stale market numbers or silently redefine a metric after seeing the result.
